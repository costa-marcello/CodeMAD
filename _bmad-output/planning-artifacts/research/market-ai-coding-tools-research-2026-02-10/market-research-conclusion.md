# Market Research Conclusion

## Summary of Key Findings

1. **The market opportunity is real.** $7.37B growing to $23.97B by 2030. The structured AI coding sub-category is being validated by well-funded players (Tessl $750M, Kiro/AWS, GitHub Spec Kit).

2. **Timing is correct.** The inflection between AI euphoria and disillusionment is happening now. Developer trust declining, code quality crisis mainstream, vibe coding backlash in full swing.

3. **The gap exists.** No competitor offers the full stack: methodology + multi-agent orchestration + desktop-native + privacy-first + tool-agnostic. Each competitor covers 1-2 dimensions.

4. **The target audience is underserved.** Quality Guardians (experienced devs who value correctness and maintainability) have no tool that addresses their needs. They are the highest-influence segment in team adoption.

5. **The protocol is the moat.** Technology features will be replicated. A community-validated methodology is the only sustainable differentiator against $29.3B-funded competitors.
