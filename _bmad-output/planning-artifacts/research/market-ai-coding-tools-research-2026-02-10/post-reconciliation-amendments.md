# Post-Reconciliation Amendments

**Date:** February 10, 2026

The market research above made four key strategic recommendations. Costa has now resolved all four. These reconciliation decisions supersede the original recommendations for CodeMAD's planning and implementation phases.

## R1 - Target Audience
**Original Recommendation:** Target Quality Guardians (experienced developers who value correctness and architecture) as beachhead segment.

**Costa's Decision:** Experienced vibecoders and developers with any experience level. The autonomous AI coding trend means even 5+ year developers use autopilot. Excludes non-technical founders (Bolt/Lovable territory). This is broader than 'Quality Guardians only' but narrower than 'everyone.'

**Rationale:** The Quality Guardian focus was too narrow given market trends showing experienced developers increasingly adopting autopilot-style workflows. The revised target captures this broader reality while maintaining a developer-focused positioning.

## R2 - Revenue Model
**Original Recommendation:** Free tier + $15-20/month paid tier at launch.

**Costa's Decision:** Fully free at launch (BYOK model) to get traction. Paid tier introduced later once traction proves what users value. Architecture includes hooks for future gating.

**Rationale:** Prioritise adoption and validation over immediate monetisation. The BYOK model removes friction for early adopters while maintaining flexibility to introduce paid features once value is demonstrated in the market.

## R3 - Launch Strategy
**Original Recommendation:** Closed beta with 20-50 trusted developers (not stealth, not public).

**Costa's Decision:** Staged stealth progression:
- Costa-only alpha (v0.1)
- 5-10 hand-picked testers at beta (v0.2)
- 20-50 testers at v0.2 expansion
- Public launch at v0.3+

**Rationale:** More conservative than the research recommendation but maintains the principle of iterative validation. Allows for tighter quality control in early phases while building toward the research-recommended closed beta size.

## R4 - Token Budgets
**Note:** Token budgets were not discussed in this market research document. This item was included in the reconciliation brief but does not apply to market research. Token budget decisions are documented in the technical research document.

## Impact on Market Research Conclusions

The reconciliation decisions maintain the strategic core of the market research findings while adjusting tactical execution:

1. **Target audience expansion** (R1) aligns with the documented trend toward autonomous coding across experience levels
2. **Free-first monetisation** (R2) reduces launch friction while preserving the ability to implement the recommended $15-20/month tier later
3. **Staged stealth approach** (R3) is more conservative than recommended but preserves the feedback loop principle
4. **Protocol-first positioning** remains unchanged and is strengthened by Costa's decisions

All four reconciliation decisions are consistent with the research's core finding: CodeMAD's methodology-driven positioning fills a genuine market gap in the structured AI coding space.

## Identified Gaps

The following gaps were identified through reconciliation analysis between brainstorming assumptions and research findings. These items require action in marketing, competitive monitoring, or validation work.

**[POSITIONING] AI code quality crisis data**
PRs up 150%, bugs up 9%, 1.7x more issues, 2.74x more security vulnerabilities, doubled code churn, 19% slower experienced devs with AI. This quantitative evidence should be used in positioning CodeMAD's protocol as the solution. Not in brainstorming. Source: Market research competitive analysis.

**[POSITIONING] Trust crisis as market signal**
Developer trust dropped from 43% to 33%. "Usage up, trust down" is the defining market signal. Brainstorming focused on speed perception and output quality but missed the broader trust collapse. CodeMAD's methodology directly addresses this. Source: Market research customer behaviour section.

**[POSITIONING] Open-source sustainability crisis**
60% of maintainers work unpaid, almost half are solo, 60% have quit or considered quitting. Kubernetes Ingress NGINX ending support due to burnout. Brainstorming acknowledged "Can Costa sustain this?" but did not connect to the broader pattern. Informs the R2 revenue decision (free now, paid later). Source: Market research risk assessment.

**[COMPETITIVE WATCH] Spec-driven competition emerging**
Tessl at $750M valuation, Kiro with 250K+ users, Spec Kit with 50K+ stars. These validate the structured methodology category but could close CodeMAD's gap. Brainstorming treated the methodology gap as uncontested -- it is not. CodeMAD's advantage is being uniquely integrated (methodology + orchestration + desktop + privacy + tool-agnostic), not uniquely methodological. Source: Market research spec-driven development section.

**[COMPETITIVE WATCH] Developer decision journey / "week one wall"**
Detailed customer journey mapping shows most free-to-paid conversions happen in week one. The 30-minute first-use window is existentially important. Brainstorming proposed a "viral moment" but did not model how developers actually discover, evaluate, and adopt tools. Architecture must optimise for first-30-minutes experience. Source: Market research customer decision processes section.

**[VALIDATE] Visual brainstorming surface as marketing screenshot**
Brainstorming: "Interactive mind maps during Phase 1. The screenshot that sells CodeMAD." Market research neither validated nor invalidated this. Needs user testing. Source: Brainstorming session, unvalidated by market research.

**[VALIDATE] Code quality score (0-100) as differentiator**
Brainstorming proposed it, market research confirms no competitor does it. But no market data on whether developers would pay for or prioritise a quality score. Needs validation. Source: Brainstorming session, gap noted in market research.

**[VALIDATE] Protocol certification badge**
"Built with CodeMAD Protocol" badge. Market research mentions methodology certification as a long-term opportunity but does not validate market demand. Domain research adds that the badge signals both quality methodology AND IP protection (human authorship evidence). Source: Brainstorming session, cross-referenced with domain research.

**[MARKETING ANGLE] EU AI Act compliance as marketing angle**
Discovered in domain research: CodeMAD's protocol naturally satisfies EU AI Act transparency requirements (Aug 2, 2026 deadline). No competitor positions on regulatory compliance. This is an untapped marketing angle. Source: Domain research regulatory section.
