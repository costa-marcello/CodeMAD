# Market Size and Growth Dynamics

## Current Market Size (2025)

The AI coding tools market is valued at **$7.37 billion in 2025** and is forecast to reach **$23.97 billion by 2030**, advancing at a **26.60% CAGR**. Developers are spending **$15 billion globally** on AI coding tools.

_Source: [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-code-tools-market-report), [Virtue Market Research](https://virtuemarketresearch.com/report/ai-developer-tools-market)_

## Market Concentration

The top 3 players (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Windsurf) capture **70%+ market share** in the coding AI agents and copilots segment. This is a winner-take-most market with extreme concentration at the top.

_Source: [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/coding-ai-market-share-december-2025/)_
