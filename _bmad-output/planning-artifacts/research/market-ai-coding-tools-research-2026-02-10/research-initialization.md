# Research Initialization

## Research Understanding Confirmed

**Topic**: AI coding tools and AI-assisted software development -- full landscape
**Goals**:
1. Validate that CodeMAD's methodology-driven positioning has a real gap to fill
2. Competitive intelligence to inform stealth strategy
3. Data to guide release layering (v0.1 through v1.0)
4. Evidence for or against key decisions (AGPL-3.0, "everyone" target, stealth launch, free model)

**Research Type**: Market Research
**Date**: 2026-02-10
**Scope Confirmed**: Yes

## Research Scope

**Geographic Focus:** Global

**Market Analysis Focus Areas:**

- Market size, growth projections, and dynamics (current + forward-looking)
- Customer segments, behaviour patterns, and spending insights
- Deep competitive landscape with individual competitor profiles
- Methodology-driven vs code-generation approaches gap analysis
- Strategic recommendations and positioning guidance

**Competitor Research Strategy:**

Deep-dive profiling using parallel research agents across all major AI coding tool categories:
- IDE-native tools (Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot, Amazon Q Developer)
- Browser/cloud builders (Bolt, Lovable, Replit, v0)
- CLI/terminal tools (Claude Code, Aider, Continue)
- Desktop applications (direct CodeMAD competitors)
- Methodology/workflow tools (if any exist)

**Key Validation Questions:**

1. Does a methodology-driven approach have precedent or demand in this market?
2. Who is actually paying for AI coding tools, and what are they paying for?
3. What's the gap between "generate code fast" and "ship working products"?
4. Is AGPL-3.0 a barrier or a non-issue for the target audience?
5. Does stealth-then-launch work in this space, or does build-in-public win?

**Research Methodology:**

- Current web data with source verification
- Multiple independent sources for critical claims
- Confidence level assessment for uncertain data
- Comprehensive coverage with no critical gaps
- 10 parallel research agents for competitor deep-dives
