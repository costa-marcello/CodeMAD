# Risk Assessment and Mitigation

## Market Risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **Well-funded competitors replicate features** | High | Very High | Lead with protocol (harder to copy). Ship fast. Build community moat. |
| **Market consolidates around 2-3 winners** | High | High | Target underserved niche (Quality Guardians). Desktop-native differentiator. |
| **Developer tool fatigue** | Medium | Medium | Position as methodology, not "another AI tool." Reduce tool switching cost. |
| **AI code quality improves rapidly** | High | Medium | Protocol value extends beyond quality (planning, analysis, architecture). Quality is the entry point, not the entire value. |
| **Desktop apps lose relevance** | Medium | Low | Multi-platform strategy hedges this. Desktop-first, not desktop-only. |

## Competitive Risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **Cursor adds structured workflows** | High | High | Protocol depth (four phases) is harder to add than lightweight spec files. Community methodology is stickier than features. |
| **Claude Code adds methodology** | High | Medium | Anthropic builds infrastructure, not methodology. CodeMAD's value is opinionated workflow, not generic capability. |
| **Kiro gains spec-driven market** | Medium | High | Kiro is AWS-locked. CodeMAD is cloud-agnostic. Kiro uses EARS notation (academic), CodeMAD uses BMAD (practitioner-friendly). |
| **GitHub Spec Kit becomes standard** | Medium | Medium | Spec Kit is scaffolding, not a platform. CodeMAD integrates methodology + tooling + orchestration. |
| **New well-funded entrant** | High | Medium | Speed to market. Community lock-in. Protocol as defensible moat. |

## Operational Risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **Solo founder burnout** | Critical | High | Revenue plan before v1.0. Scope ruthlessly. Accept help (contributors, co-founder). |
| **AGPL blocks enterprise adoption** | Medium | Medium | Dual licensing model. AGPL is correct for beachhead. Commercial licence when enterprise demand emerges. |
| **Tauri ecosystem immaturity** | Medium | Low | Tauri 2.0 is stable. Community growing 35% YoY. Fallback to Electron exists if needed. |
| **EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance** | Medium | Medium | Open-source "steward" obligations require monitoring. Budget for compliance as product grows. |

## Risk Summary

The three highest-impact risks are:
1. **Solo founder burnout** -- the research overwhelmingly shows this is the #1 killer of open-source developer tools. Concrete revenue plan and scope discipline are essential.
2. **Well-funded competitor replication** -- Cursor at $29.3B valuation can throw engineers at any feature. The protocol is the only sustainable moat.
3. **AI quality improvement** -- if AI code quality improves dramatically, the "quality crisis" narrative weakens. Mitigation: position the protocol as valuable for planning and architecture, not just code quality.
